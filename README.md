### Hi, I'm ChengAo Shen (沈骋骜) 👋

I build agents, both as a founder and as a researcher.

**🐱 Building.** I founded [CatVinci Studio](https://catvinci.com), where we make everyday tools built around agents, aimed at how people read, write and manage information at work. Our apps are local-first and open source, written mostly in Rust and TypeScript: [Levis](https://github.com/CatVinci-Studio/Levis) (AI-native Markdown editor), [Mneme](https://github.com/CatVinci-Studio/Mneme) (read-it-later with a knowledge agent), [Posto](https://github.com/CatVinci-Studio/Posto) (agent-powered email) and [Verko](https://github.com/CatVinci-Studio/Verko) (agent-first paper management).

**🔬 Research.** I'm a Ph.D. student in CS at the [University of Houston](https://uh.edu/), working on time series analysis and LLM-based agent systems. That covers turning large vision models into small, efficient forecasters, and agents that automate analytical work. I've published at EMNLP, NeurIPS, ACL and IJCAI. The full list is on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=aupSEN4AAAAJ&hl=en).
